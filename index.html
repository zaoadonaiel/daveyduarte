About

Two decades of building things that outlast the trend cycle.

Davey Duarte is a bondservant of Jesus Christ, husband, father of 4, creative director, entrepreneur, photographer, videographer and developer with over two decades of experience building brands, websites, and scalable tech platforms.

His portfolio spans a wide range of ventures, including custom-built apps, business platforms, and creative projects designed to solve real-world problems and drive results. From launching innovative tools like business directories and service-based platforms to developing high-performing websites for clients across industries, Davey brings ideas to life from concept to execution.

Beyond tech, Davey is also a musician, photographer, and creative storyteller, blending art with strategy in everything he builds. This site serves as a central hub for his work—showcasing the projects, businesses, and ideas he continues to develop and scale.
